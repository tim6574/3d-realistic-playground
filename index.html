LOCAL SINGLE-PLAYER

World saves include collision damage, vehicle condition, glass breakage and detached-part state. This build adds a plastically deforming body shell and periodically rebuilt convex collision hull. The chassis remains one rigid body, not a BeamNG-style node-beam solver. Online rooms use encrypted WebRTC data channels, public PeerJS signaling, and host-authoritative physics. TURN relays, reconnect recovery, authoritative player prediction, active player ragdolls, pathfinding across complex city maps and water simulation still require further work.
